# Paper originals

`cot.txt`, `tot.txt`, `gtot.txt` are the prompts as printed in Ouédraogo et al. (EMSE 31:103, 2026), Fig. 3a, Fig. 3b and Fig. 4, transcribed from the pilot instruction document of 2026-09-05 (typos kept). ZSL and FSL are not printed in the paper; the zod versions in `../ZSL.txt` and `../FSL.txt` are reconstructions marked provisional in `manifest.json`. Each file ends with one newline that is stripped at render time.
